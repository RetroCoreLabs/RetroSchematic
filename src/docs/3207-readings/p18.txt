note 18A is printed SING_DIFF_SW_P: a socket where the cable pins Y1-Y50 (E1-H7, the OSCSI bus to the SCSI connector) face both A1-A50 (A1-D7, single-ended side SINGSCSI) and B1-B50 (J1-M7, differential side DIFFSCSI). INFERRED: a plug in one of two positions joins Y to A or to B.
note The A side matches the standard single-ended SCSI pinout exactly (DB0 on pin 2 ... I/O on 50, TERMPWR on 26, odd pins and 20, 30, 34 to ground) and the B side the standard differential pinout (+DB0/-DB0 on 3/4, DIFFSENS 21, TERMPWR 25 and 26, ATN 29/30): CHECKED by comparing every column.
note The unnamed lines on the A and B sides go to one top line that ends at the ground symbol; K11 (B26) is joined to K10 DTERMPWR by a short wire. B10 (cable pin A25) has no dot on either line: OPEN (pin 25 is unused in single-ended SCSI).
note PSING (D8 = PSING~, H8 to ground) and PDIFF (M8 = PDIFF~) are the plug-position sense pins: the plug grounds PSING~ or PDIFF~. INFERRED from the drawing, the PDIFF lower pin is not drawn.
note 18A is a pin-field socket on the board (component-side photo: a small green board marked "1887A2" sits in it, the print beside it says "SING ->" above and "<- DIFF" below). The plug-in board is 350217, PCB 1887 print A2, "SCSI Single/Differential adapter" (norsk-data.com, nd-350217: used on 350004-350007, no chips). It is pushed towards SING or DIFF to choose the SCSI mode. Stored as board connector J.18A (owner's ruling 2026-11-28).
