note 5A pin 1 is printed "6TRANS~": it sits on the same wire as pin 19 STRANS~ (S and 6 look alike in this font), so it is STRANS~.
note 8A and 9A (receivers BD~ -> SDIN00-15) have both enables on one wire that ends at the ground symbol below 9A: always on. BD~16-23 are driven but not received.
