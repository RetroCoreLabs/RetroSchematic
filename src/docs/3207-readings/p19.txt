note 25B pin 10 is printed "SIGS" (checked at 800 dpi: the S is the same glyph as in SIGS at 24A pin 1).
note 23A pin 15 (EB, drive enable) is on the STGS line from 21A pin 18; EY~ (pin 1) is RSTGS = STGS OR DIFF from 25B.
note RN1, RN3 (4608X104_221/331) and RN2 (4610X104_221/331) are 220/330 ohm terminator packs: each line pin has 220 ohm to the TERMS rail (pin 8 or 10) and 330 ohm to ground (pin 1). Read from the pin numbers drawn on both resistors of each line.
note D4 (1N3246) feeds the TERMS rail from +5 V; strap STR2 (at 20A) puts it on the cable as STERMPWR.
note TH2 (2170576F) is a hex switch: D8 = ESCSI (SCSI on), D4/D2/D1 = ID~2..ID~0 (the card's SCSI ID). C to ground, C~ through R6 to +5 V.
note 13C prints LDWC without ~ here; sheet 15 lists it at the S_ND_INT box as LDWC~: OPEN which is right.
note PAL contents (24B "78200", 21A "42500", 13C "78300") not read (BUGS.md).
note LD1 "REQ" and LD2 "BUSY" are front LEDs driven by 14D from REQ and BSYIN.
