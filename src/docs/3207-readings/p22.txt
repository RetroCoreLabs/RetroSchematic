note 11F ("42801") and 10F ("42200") are TBP18S030 PROMs addressed by the thumbwheel TH1 (D1-D8 on A1-A4) and SLIDENT (A0): they give the ND device number SDEV00-15, read onto SDOUT by 11E/10B when SLIDENT~ is low. Contents not read (BUGS.md).
note 10C and 10E (25LS2521 8-bit comparators, cascaded through 10C pin 19 -> 10E pin 1, 10C enabled by SMEM) compare SDEV06-15 with SDIN06-15; 10C bits 0-3, 5 and 6 are tied to ground on both sides. The result is latched in 13A 7D when SDLDAPR clocks the address: 13A pin 16 -> 8C -> SEQUAL (device address equal).
note 13A latches SDIN00-05 as LADR0-5 and the term SDIN03 AND SINT11 (10A) in 8D; 13A 8Q decides whether this card passes IDENT on (1A: SOTIDENT~) or answers it (2A: SLIDENT). INFERRED from the gates.
note 14A (74LS138, enabled by SRIOXE, SIOEQ~ and LADR3 low) decodes LADR0-2 into the eight IOX register strobes: 0 RMARL~, 1 LMARL~, 2 SRDAT~, 3 LDAT~, 4 SRSTAT~, 5 SLOCW~, 6 RMARU~, 7 LMARU~. SIOEQ~ = NOT(26C AND LADR5 low AND SEQUAL), NCR~ = NOT(LADR5 AND SEQUAL AND 26C AND SRIOXE): LADR5 = 1 selects the NCR5386 registers.
note 26C pin 13 comes from an unnamed off-page arrow; 26D is a 74LS13 (Schmitt NAND, 4 inputs).
note The bus lines in (BAPR~ BDRY~ BINACK~ BMEM~ BMCL~ BERROR~ BIOXE~) pass the 74LS240 3B with both enables to ground; 3B input 15 is SMCL from its own output pin 9, so pin 5 gives SMCL~.
note R4 (1 kohm, H6 pull-up, grid D3) is the right R4: confirmed by Ronny 2026-11-28. The 220 ohm resistor printed "R4" on sheet 13 is stored as R47.
