note 14B ("78100" PAL20L10, "BYTE/WORD") and 15F ("78400" PAL20L8B): contents not read (BUGS.md).
note 17F and 30C are HY5010-100 delay lines (10 taps, 10-100 ns). 17F is started by SDRY~ and gives DEL50 (50 ns) and tap 90 ns to 14B; 30C is started by 29B (ADDRESS AND SLGRANT) and gives SAPR (30 ns), SAPR~ (40 ns, inverted), SEDAP~ (70 ns) and DDAP (100 ns).
note The 74S08 gate (SINT AND 15F pin 15 -> 25C) has no position code printed next to it: it is kept as U74S08 here. OPEN: which chip it is. The diamond at 29B pin 12 is printed "ADDRESS" (not SADDRESS).
note 27A (74F74) samples SNREQ on SMEM; with 5B (74F112, J to ground, K = 27A Q, clocked by INGRANT~, preset by SMEM AND SDRY~) it makes SLGRANT, the card's own bus grant; SOTGRANT~ = 27A-Q~-term OR INGRANT~ passes the grant on. INFERRED from the wiring.
note 30B pin 13 (clear) is wired to SLGRANT: the wire from pin 13 runs down, left and up to the SLGRANT dot at pin 12, crossing SDLDAPR with no dot (checked at 600 dpi).
note 28A (74LS123) pins as drawn: the trigger from 4A pin 8 enters on pin 2 (B) and pin 1 (A~) goes to ground; the drawing prints the row names A~ and B the other way round but the pin numbers match the datasheet. Timing R9 10k, C6 220 pF. Q = SMC (SCSI master clear pulse).
note 15A (74LS273, clocked by SLOCW, cleared by SMC~) is the control register: SDIN03 TEST, SDIN05 SNDMAEN, SDIN06 TOMEM, SDIN08 FDIFF, SDIN09 FSING, SDIN10 TRST; SDIN07 and SDIN11 are latched to unused outputs.
note 28C here uses 3->4 (SDMARES), 5->6, 1->2 and 9->8; so on sheet 21 only 13->12 is left, which proves its printed input "3" is pin 13.
